Before you start

Three things to know about every product.

  • Your own address. Every school or coaching has an address like yourname.nextsoftwares.in. Open it and the login page already knows which school you are signing in to.
  • You must be added first. Only people the owner or admin has added can log in. Ask your admin to add your mobile number (gyms, salons, clinics) or e-mail (schools and coaching).
  • One-time codes. Every sign-in sends an OTP to the registered number or e-mail. Nobody needs to remember a password for the daily login.
Use a recent version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. On a phone, you can add the page to the home screen to open it like an app.

NextEdge — schools and coaching

Open yourschool.nextsoftwares.in, or go to the login page, choose NextEdge and search by the school’s name.

Log in

  1. Enter the e-mail registered with the school.
  2. Type the OTP sent to that e-mail.
  3. You land on Today, the day’s attendance, fees and alerts at a glance.

What you will find in the menu

  • Students, Batches, Timetable, Attendance — admissions, classes and daily attendance.
  • Fees — fee heads, collection, receipts and dues. Payroll and Staff attendance for the staff side.
  • Exams & report cards, Online tests, Certificates, ID cards — results and documents.
  • Notices, Homework, Messaging, Library, Study material — day-to-day communication and resources.
  • Enquiries — admission leads and follow-ups. Reports & graphs for the owner.
  • Staff & privileges — add teachers and office staff and choose what each person can open.

First week checklist

  1. Fill the school profile and logo.
  2. Add staff and give each person only the sections they need.
  3. Create batches / classes, then import or add students.
  4. Set the fee structure and start collecting.

NextFit — gyms and fitness studios

Open fit.nextsoftwares.in and sign in with the mobile number registered at your gym.

Log in

  1. Enter your registered mobile number.
  2. Type the OTP.
  3. You see only the screens your role allows: owner, manager, front desk, accountant or trainer.

Everyday tasks

  • Members and plans — add a member, start a membership, renew it and send a reminder.
  • Fees — record payments and split a fee into instalments.
  • Leads and referrals — note enquiries, follow them up and convert them into members.
  • Feedback — ask members for feedback after a visit.
  • Expenses and profit report — owner and accountant only.

NextGlow — salons and spas

Open glow.nextsoftwares.in and sign in with the mobile number registered at your salon.

Everyday tasks

  • Customers and services — keep the customer list and the service menu with prices.
  • Memberships and packages — sell a package, track its use and renew it.
  • Staff — add stylists and front-desk staff with the right role.
  • Leads, referrals, feedback — follow up enquiries and collect feedback.
  • Expenses and profit report — owner and accountant only.
Login works exactly like NextFit: registered mobile number, then OTP.

NextCare — clinics and labs

Open care.nextsoftwares.in and sign in with the mobile number registered at your clinic.

Everyday tasks

  • Patients and services — keep the patient list and the services you offer.
  • Plans and follow-up reminders — sell a care plan and remind patients to come back.
  • Staff — add doctors, reception and accounts staff with the right role.
  • Leads, referrals, feedback — follow up enquiries and collect feedback.
  • Expenses and profit report — owner and accountant only.
Login works exactly like NextFit: registered mobile number, then OTP.

Next Era — hotels and resorts

Next Era has its own app. Choose Next Era on the login page and press Open Next Era login.

  • One login per person; staff only see their own department.
  • Front desk, housekeeping, restaurant, banquet, stores and accounts in one place.
  • Staff attendance, tasks and approvals; GST invoices and an owner dashboard on the phone.

To see how it works for your property, ask for a demo at nextresource.in/next-era.

Problems?

  • OTP did not arrive. Check the number or e-mail is the registered one, wait a minute and ask again.
  • “No workspace has this address”. Check the spelling of the address, or search the school’s name on the login page.
  • You are not allowed to open a screen. Ask the owner or admin to change your role.
  • Still stuck. Write to hello@nextresource.in with your product, the school or outlet name and a screenshot.
