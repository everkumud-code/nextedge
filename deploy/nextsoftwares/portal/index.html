How logging in works

The same three steps for every product.

Help

Quick answers. If these do not solve it, write to hello@nextresource.in and tell us your product and your school / outlet name.

I do not remember my school’s address

Choose NextEdge above and search by the school or coaching name. The result shows the address; you can also ask your school office.

I did not receive the OTP

Check that the mobile number or e-mail is the one registered with your school or outlet, wait one minute, and request the code again. Codes are valid for a few minutes only.

My number or e-mail is not accepted

Only people added by the school or outlet owner can log in. Ask the owner or admin to add you under Staff, then try again.

Where does the owner or the NextSoftwares team log in?

Click Team login at the top right. Gym, salon and clinic owners use the NextFit / NextGlow / NextCare login with their mobile number.

Is my data separate from other schools or outlets?

Yes. Every workspace has its own address and its own data. People in one workspace cannot open another.
